MeanTime

Micro-ads during AI wait time. You keep 55%.

How MeanTime works

  • A small, labelled text ad appears under your prompt while the AI answers. It never covers the page — keep typing, scrolling, and clicking as usual.
  • 5 seconds of real view time earns the view. The timer runs only while the ad is on screen, the tab is visible, and the window is focused; switch away and it pauses.
  • You keep 55% of what the advertiser pays, credited per verified view.
  • Your prompts stay private. MeanTime never reads, stores, or sends what you type.

Permissions: storage keeps your session, settings, and cached ads; alarms syncs earnings; idle prefetches while you are away. On AI sites it reads page structure only, to place the ad.

Sign in to start earning. Ads appear only after you send an AI prompt, and you can pause them at any time.
